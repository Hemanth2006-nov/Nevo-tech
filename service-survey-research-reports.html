— FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about our questionnaire design, survey statistics, and university thesis reports.

Q. Can you help design our questionnaire from scratch?

Yes! We structure validated questionnaires featuring demographic items, behavioral filters, and 5-point Likert scale questions directly aligned with your research questions and hypotheses.

Q. What statistical tests and software packages are supported?

We provide complete quantitative computing using SPSS, Advanced Excel, Python, and R — including Descriptive Statistics (Mean, SD), Chi-Square tests, Independent T-Tests, One-Way ANOVA, Correlation, and Multiple Regression.

Q. Do you supply the raw Excel survey data alongside the report?

Yes! You receive the full raw response dataset, cleaned spreadsheets, SPSS output tables with p-values, high-resolution chart images, and complete mathematical appendices.

Q. Are university formatting and citation standards strictly followed?

Yes. Every thesis and manuscript is formatted strictly according to APA, Harvard, or your college's official guidelines with formatted references, literature review citations, and defense PPT slides.
